Lighthouse Expands to BSD City

Lighthouse International Learning Center was founded by a couple, Erik G. Widjanarko and Lanny Elham, in their humble home around 2010. Their love and passion for teaching grew from homeschooling their four children. What began as a small after-school tutoring center at home eventually developed into an officially established International Learning Center located in Permata Buana, West Jakarta. Today, Lighthouse International Learning Center PerBun serves over 500 students.

In light of this growth, we have decided to expand by opening a second branch in BSD City — bringing the same trusted bimbel (tutoring centre) experience families know from Permata Buana to parents and students across BSD City and Tangerang Selatan.

Why Lighthouse BSD?

As a trusted bimbel and international tutoring centre in BSD City, we do more than help your child get better grades — we build genuine academic confidence.

  • Aligned with Your Child's School Curriculum

    We teach Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel, and IB syllabi — fully aligned with what your child learns and is assessed on at school.

  • Personalised Assessment from Day One

    Every student begins with a comprehensive assessment, allowing us to identify key focus areas and share clear insights with parents.

  • Supplemental Lessons

    We provide additional enrichment lessons at no extra cost for students who need extra support, based on teacher recommendation and availability.

  • 14+ Years of Proven Experience

    Lighthouse has been guiding students since 2010 across multiple campuses, with hundreds of success stories behind us.
